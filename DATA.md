# Data availability

This clean public release contains code and compact detector metrics. It does
not redistribute research datasets or row-level evaluation artifacts.

## Included

- dataset loaders and preprocessing code;
- detector training and evaluation scripts;
- guided and unguided generation utilities;
- automatic evaluation and sensitivity-analysis code;
- compact detector training histories.

## Excluded

- raw or derived research datasets;
- individual model generations and evaluator responses;
- survey exports and per-rater human-evaluation rows;
- checkpoints and model weights;
- runtime logs and machine snapshots;
- manuscripts, submission packages, and private source archives.

Obtain HateXplain, STATE ToxiCN, and K-HATERS from their official releases and
verify their terms before use. Configure local input and output paths rather
than committing copies to this repository.
